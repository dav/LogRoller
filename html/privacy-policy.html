Privacy Policy for LogRoller

Effective date: March 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LogRoller ("the App") handles information when you use the macOS desktop app, the logroller CLI, or the HTTPS ingest server.

1. Information We Collect

The App operates entirely on your local machine. We may collect:

  • Device and technical information such as operating system version, app version, and crash diagnostics (via Apple's standard crash reporting).
  • Information you provide directly when contacting us for support.

The App does not collect analytics, usage data, or telemetry. All event data is stored locally on your machine and is never transmitted to external servers.

2. Local Network Communication

LogRoller runs an HTTPS server on localhost (port 8443) that accepts event data from clients on your local machine or local network. No data is transmitted to external servers. All ingested events are stored as NDJSON files in your local Application Support directory.

3. Event Data

Events sent to LogRoller by your applications are stored locally and are under your full control. The App does not inspect, modify, or forward event payloads. You can delete all stored data by removing the LogRoller directory from Application Support or by uninstalling the App.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App.
  • Diagnose and fix bugs and crashes.
  • Respond to support requests.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information with third parties. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

6. Data Retention

The App stores event data and preferences locally on your machine. We do not retain any data on external servers. You can remove all local data by uninstalling the App.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
